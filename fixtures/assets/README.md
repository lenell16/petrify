# Fixture media provenance

The `fieldnotes` account, captions, shortcodes and media IDs are fictional. Its five photos are NASA Image and Video Library images downloaded as `~medium.jpg` from the following records:

| Local file | NASA record |
| --- | --- |
| `0300804.jpg` | [Space Science / Aurora Australis](https://images.nasa.gov/details/0300804) |
| `iss045e013851.jpg` | [Earth Observation from the International Space Station](https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss045e013851) |
| `S39-23-020.jpg` | [Aurora Australis, Sinuous Loop](https://images.nasa.gov/details/S39-23-020) |
| `S39-23-036.jpg` | [Aurora Australis, Red Crown](https://images.nasa.gov/details/S39-23-036) |
| `iss072e083078.jpg` | [The aurora australis streams over the Earth](https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss072e083078) |

`motion.mp4` and `earth.mp4` are short, silent H.264 pans across those photos, generated by `swift fixtures/generate-media.swift`; they are not Instagram downloads. NASA is credited as the source, not as an endorser of Petrify. The [NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) say NASA content is generally not subject to US copyright and may be used for informational purposes with acknowledgement; NASA logos and third-party-credited material have separate restrictions. No NASA logos or identifiable people are used here. All fixture routes serve only these bundled bytes; no browser request to NASA or Instagram is needed.
